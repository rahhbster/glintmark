Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

The short version: Glintmark does not collect your personal information, does not track you, and does not sell data. Your cards live on your device.

Who we are

Glintmark is an iOS app published by HZHLLC. It lets you create, save, and share quote cards.

What data we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or login. Glintmark requires no sign-up and stores no user profile anywhere.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising identifiers.
  • No location data. We never request location access.
  • No contacts or calendar access.

Where your cards are stored

All cards you create — including text, background choices, and style settings — are stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage. They are never uploaded to a server or synchronized to any cloud service by Glintmark.

If you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings, your device backups (which may include Glintmark data) are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Photos and camera

If you choose to use a photo from your library as a card background, Glintmark requests read access to your Photos library. This image is loaded directly onto your card and stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

You can revoke photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

The AI quote parser

Glintmark can use an AI service to pull the quote text, attribution, and source out of text you paste or share into the app. It runs in two cases:

  • When you tap Paste & Parse.
  • When you share text into Glintmark from another app and the app can't recognize the quote on its own, but only if you have allowed AI parsing. Glintmark asks the first time, and you can turn it off in Settings → “Use AI to read quotes”.

When it runs:

  • By default, the text is sent to our server (glintmark-api.vercel.app), which forwards it to OpenRouter to be parsed and returns the result. Our server does not store the text. Like any web server, it sees your device's IP address for the duration of the request.
  • If you enter your own OpenRouter API key in Settings, the text goes directly from your device to OpenRouter instead, and never reaches our server. Your key is stored in your device's Keychain and is sent only to OpenRouter. Parses then bill your OpenRouter account: a free model is tried first, with a low-cost paid model as fallback (well under $0.001 per parse).
  • Only the text is sent — not your name, device ID, location, cards, or any other personal identifier.
  • OpenRouter's handling of requests is governed by their privacy policy.

If you'd rather no text leave your device, type your quotes in the app instead of pasting or sharing them in.

Card sharing and export

When you export or share a card, Glintmark renders it as an image file and hands it to iOS's standard share sheet. Where that image goes — whether to Instagram, Messages, Photos, or anywhere else — is your choice and governed by those platforms' policies, not ours.

Third-party services

Beyond the optional AI parser described above, Glintmark does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Background photos are bundled inside the app and stay on your device. Choosing or exporting one makes no network request. The photos come from Unsplash; the photographer credit links in Settings open Unsplash in your browser only if you tap them.

When the app starts, it downloads a list of authors that have portrait cards from our own server (glintmark-api.vercel.app). If a card uses the Portrait theme, the app downloads that author's portrait image from Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org, which serves the file from Wikimedia) when you open or export the card. These are standard network requests; we do not control or have access to Wikipedia's server logs.

Children's privacy

Glintmark does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will note them in the App Store release notes.
